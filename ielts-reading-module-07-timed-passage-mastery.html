Module 7: Timed Passage Mastery

Combine paraphrase recognition, evidence location and question-type control under time pressure.

Strategy Lesson

What to do

  1. Preview the question set first.
  2. Use targeted scanning rather than reading every line equally.
  3. Skip and return if one item is taking too long.
  4. Review errors by cause, not just score.
Practice Passage

Researchers examining workplace concentration found that short periods of uninterrupted work were often more productive than longer sessions containing frequent digital interruptions. However, the benefit varied according to task complexity and individual preference.

Guided Practice

1. Short uninterrupted periods were sometimes more productive than interrupted longer sessions.

2. The effect was identical for all workers and tasks.

Mastery Check

1. Digital interruptions can reduce productivity.

2. Task complexity may influence the effect.

3. The study proved that everyone should work in exactly the same way.
