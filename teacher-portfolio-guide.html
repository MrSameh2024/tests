Teaching Portfolio Guide

دليل بناء ملف الإنجاز المهني للمعلم

A teaching portfolio is a selected collection of evidence that shows how you teach, how your students learn, and how you grow. A good portfolio is useful for appraisal, promotion, job applications, awards and your own reflection.

ملف الإنجاز المهني مجموعة مختارة من الأدلة تُظهر كيف تدرّس، وكيف يتعلم طلابك، وكيف تتطور مهنيًا. يفيدك في تقييم الأداء والترقية والتقدم للوظائف والجوائز، وفي تأملك الذاتي.

Recommended sections · الأقسام المقترحة

SectionWhat to include
1. Introduction and teaching philosophy · فلسفتي في التدريسA one-page statement of what you believe about learning and how it shows in your classroom.
2. Professional profile · الملف المهنيShort CV, qualifications, subjects and grades taught, roles and responsibilities.
3. Planning · التخطيطTwo or three lesson plans or unit plans you are proud of, with a short reflection on each.
4. Teaching and learning · التدريس والتعلمPhotos of classroom displays or activities (no student faces without permission), materials you created, differentiated resources.
5. Assessment and student progress · التقويم وتقدم الطلابRubrics, formative assessment examples, anonymised student work before and after, class results with a short analysis.
6. Professional development · التطوير المهنيCertificates, workshops attended or delivered, action research summaries, observation feedback.
7. Contribution to the school · الإسهام في المدرسةCommittees, clubs, events, mentoring colleagues, community links.
8. Reflection and goals · التأمل والأهدافYour growth plan and what you learned this year.

Make every piece of evidence count · اجعل لكل دليل قيمة

A portfolio is not a box of papers. Add a short caption to each item using three questions:

What?What is this item? "A differentiated worksheet for a Grade 10 reading lesson."
So what?What does it show about my teaching or my students' learning? "It let all three groups answer the same main-idea question; 24 of 28 students completed it."
Now what?What will I do next because of it? "I will plan the extension task more carefully, because the fast finishers completed it in five minutes."
  • Quality over quantity: 15–25 strong items are better than 100 weak ones.
  • Show impact: wherever possible, include evidence of student learning, not only of your activity.
  • Protect privacy: remove student names and faces, or get written permission.
  • Keep it current: add two or three items each term rather than everything at the end of the year.

أضف لكل دليل تعليقًا قصيرًا يجيب عن: ما هذا؟ وماذا يُظهر عن تدريسي وتعلم طلابي؟ وماذا سأفعل بعده؟ الجودة أهم من الكم، واحذف أسماء الطلاب وصورهم.

A digital portfolio for free · ملف رقمي مجاني

  • A shared drive folder (Google Drive or OneDrive) with one sub-folder per section: the simplest option.
  • A free website builder (Google Sites, or a free WordPress.com site) if you want a page you can share with a link.
  • One PDF exported at the end of the year for applications and appraisal meetings.

If you need the portfolio for a licence, promotion or award, always check the current official requirements with your school or the relevant authority.
