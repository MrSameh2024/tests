Module 2: Part 1 Natural Answers

Answer familiar questions directly, naturally and with enough development.

Strategy Lesson
  • Answer the exact question first.
  • Add one clear reason or explanation.
  • Support with a natural example or detail.
  • Keep speaking rather than searching for perfect wording.
Practice Prompt

Do you prefer mornings or evenings?

Prepare briefly, then speak without reading a written answer.
