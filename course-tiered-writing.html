على أي أساس؟ · Three bases

الاستعداد · Readiness: مستوى الطالب الحالي في الكتابة، وهو أساس المستويات.

الاهتمام · Interest: اختيار الموضوع: رياضة، تقنية، تطوع.

نمط التعلم · Profile: فردي أو جماعي، ورقي أو على المنصة.

قاعدة ذهبية: الهدف نفسه، والطالب ينتقل بين المستويات، والأسماء محايدة (ألوان لا تصنيفات).
Same goal · flexible movement · neutral labels.

أمثلة أنواع الكتابة الخمسة مع نصوص نموذجية · Five genres with model texts

1البريد الإلكتروني غير الرسمي · Informal Email

الهدف · Objective: Students will write an informal email to a friend describing an event.
موضوعات الدورة: اليوم الرياضي · اليوم الوطني · سباق الهجن

دعم · Support دورة السقالات + المحطات 1–5

Hi Sara, How are you? Guess what! Last week I went to the National Day celebration with my family. It was amazing because we saw the fireworks. Write back soon, Mona

أساسي · Core المحطات 6–8

Hi Sara, I hope you’re well. Last Thursday my family and I celebrated National Day. First, we watched a traditional dance, and then we tried karak and balaleet. In the evening, fireworks lit up the sky over the Corniche. It was the best night of the year! Write back soon, Mona

إثراء · Extension المحطتان 9–10 + تحدٍّ

You won’t believe how fast the camels were – the crowd was louder than thunder! Why don’t you join us at the next race?

Main course · الدورة الرئيسيةScaffolded course · دورة السقالات
2خطاب التقديم لوظيفة · Cover Letter

الهدف · Objective: Students will write a formal cover letter that matches their skills to a job advert.
موضوعات الدورة: مهندس أمن سيبراني · موظف استقبال فندق · مبرمج ذكاء اصطناعي

دعم · Support دورة السقالات + المحطات 1–5

Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to apply for the position of hotel receptionist. I have experience in helping visitors at school events. I am polite and organised. I look forward to hearing from you.

أساسي · Core المحطات 6–8

Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to apply for the receptionist position advertised on your website. Last year I volunteered at a sports event, where I welcomed guests and answered their questions. I speak Arabic and English fluently, and I work well under pressure. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application. Yours faithfully,

إثراء · Extension المحطتان 9–10 + تحدٍّ

During my summer internship, I built a simple chatbot that answered customers’ questions, which shows my skills in coding and problem-solving.

Main course · الدورة الرئيسيةScaffolded course · دورة السقالات
3القصة الشخصية · Personal Narrative

الهدف · Objective: Students will write a personal narrative in the past tenses with a clear sequence and a lesson.
موضوعات الدورة: نقطة تحول · مساعدة الآخرين · التطوع · التغلب على تحدٍّ

دعم · Support دورة السقالات + المحطات 1–5

One day, I volunteered at a charity market. At first, I felt shy. Then, I helped an old man carry his bags. In the end, I felt proud. I learned that small actions matter.

أساسي · Core المحطات 6–8

Last winter, I decided to volunteer at a charity market in my neighbourhood. At first, I was nervous because I didn’t know anyone. However, after I helped an elderly man with his shopping, he smiled and thanked me warmly. That moment changed how I see volunteering: it is not only about giving, but also about receiving kindness.

إثراء · Extension المحطتان 9–10 + تحدٍّ

“Are you sure you can do this?” my coach asked. By the time the race started, I had already trained for three months.

Main course · الدورة الرئيسيةScaffolded course · دورة السقالات
4مقال المزايا والعيوب · Advantages & Disadvantages Essay

الهدف · Objective: Students will write a balanced essay with advantages, disadvantages and a reasoned conclusion.
موضوعات الدورة: السفر · الفعاليات الدولية · الحيوانات الأليفة · التسوق الإلكتروني

دعم · Support دورة السقالات + المحطات 1–5

Nowadays, many people shop online. One advantage is that it saves time. Another benefit is that prices are often lower. However, one disadvantage is that we cannot try clothes on. In conclusion, I believe online shopping is useful.

أساسي · Core المحطات 6–8

Online shopping has become part of daily life. On the one hand, it saves time and offers more choice than local shops. On the other hand, customers may receive damaged items or spend more than they planned. Overall, online shopping is helpful if people shop carefully and use trusted websites.

إثراء · Extension المحطتان 9–10 + تحدٍّ

In conclusion, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks: when Qatar hosted the FIFA World Cup 2022, online ticketing and delivery apps made life easier for millions of visitors.

Main course · الدورة الرئيسيةScaffolded course · دورة السقالات
5النص الإجرائي · Procedural Writing

الهدف · Objective: Students will explain a process in clear steps using imperatives and sequencing words.
موضوعات الدورة: خطوات إعداد شطيرة

دعم · Support دورة السقالات + المحطات 1–5

First, take two slices of bread. Next, spread the butter. After that, add cheese and tomato. Finally, close the sandwich.

أساسي · Core المحطات 6–8

To make a healthy sandwich, you need bread, cheese, tomato and lettuce. First, wash the vegetables carefully. Next, cut the tomato into thin slices. After that, put the cheese, tomato and lettuce on one slice of bread. Finally, cover it with the second slice and cut it in half.

إثراء · Extension المحطتان 9–10 + تحدٍّ

How to create a safe email account: First, choose a password with at least 12 characters. Make sure you never share it. Warning: do not click links from unknown senders.

Main course · الدورة الرئيسيةScaffolded course · دورة السقالات
