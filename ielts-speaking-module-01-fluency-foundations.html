Strategy Lesson

1. The 3-Step Expansion Pattern

For many Part 1 questions, avoid one-line answers. Use:

  1. Answer — respond directly.
  2. Reason — explain why.
  3. Example / Detail — add one natural detail.
Question: Do you enjoy reading?

Weak: Yes, I do.

Stronger: Yes, especially non-fiction. I enjoy it because I can learn something practical, and I usually read for about twenty minutes before bed.

2. Reduce Hesitation

  • Do not search for a perfect word; paraphrase and continue.
  • Use natural linking phrases such as because, for example, especially, although, so.
  • A short pause is better than repeated fillers.
  • Keep the answer moving even if the grammar is not perfect.
Goal: clear, connected speech—not memorised perfection.
