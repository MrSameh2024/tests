Module 5: Sentence & Summary Completion

Predict grammar, respect the word limit and copy evidence accurately.

Strategy

  1. Read the sentence before the gap.
  2. Predict the grammar and meaning needed.
  3. Respect the word limit exactly.
  4. Copy spelling accurately from the passage.

Practice Passage

The research team installed sensors in public parks to measure temperature and humidity. Data were collected every ten minutes for six months.

Guided Practice

1. The team installed ______ in public parks.

2. Data were collected every ______ minutes.

Mastery Check

1. The sensors measured temperature and ______.

2. The study lasted for ______ months.

3. The equipment was placed in public ______.
