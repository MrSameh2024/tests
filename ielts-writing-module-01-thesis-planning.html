Module 1: Position, Thesis & Paragraph Planning

Plan before you write. Make your position visible from the start.

خطط قبل أن تكتب، واجعل موقفك واضحًا منذ البداية.

Strategy Lesson

1. The Three Decisions Before Writing

  1. What exactly is the question asking?
  2. What is my position?
  3. What two main ideas will support that position?
Common problem: students start writing before deciding their position, then the essay becomes inconsistent.
Worked Example

2. Worked Example

Prompt: Some people think university students should study only subjects related to their future careers. Others believe they should be free to study any subject they choose. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Position: Students should have freedom to choose, but career relevance should still be considered.

Thesis: Although career-focused courses can improve employability, I believe students should retain substantial freedom to study subjects that match their interests and strengths.

Paragraph 1: Benefits of career-focused study.
Paragraph 2: Why broader choice remains valuable.
