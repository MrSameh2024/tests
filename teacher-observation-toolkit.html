Classroom Observation Toolkit

أدوات ملاحظة الحصص وتبادل الزيارات الصفية

Peer observation is one of the most practical ways to improve teaching: you see new ideas in action and get feedback on your own lessons. This toolkit is for developmental observation between colleagues. It is not an appraisal tool.

تبادل الزيارات الصفية من أكثر طرق تحسين التدريس عملية: ترى أفكارًا جديدة مطبقة وتحصل على تغذية راجعة عن حصصك. هذه الأدوات للملاحظة التطويرية بين الزملاء، وليست أداة لتقييم الأداء الوظيفي.

The observation cycle · دورة الملاحظة

  1. Before (10 minutes) · قبل الحصة
    The teacher being observed chooses one focus (for example: questioning, instructions, pair work, feedback, differentiation) and explains the class context and lesson aim.
  2. During the lesson · أثناء الحصة
    The observer records only what they see and hear related to the focus: teacher and student words, actions, timings. No judgements yet.
  3. After (within 48 hours) · بعد الحصة
    The teacher reflects first. Then the observer shares evidence and asks questions. Both agree one next step.

Ground rules: agree the focus together · notes belong to the observed teacher · no student names · talk about the teaching, not the teacher · say thank you.

قواعد أساسية: اتفقا على محور واحد، والملاحظات ملك للمعلم المُلاحَظ، ولا أسماء للطلاب، والحديث عن التدريس لا عن الشخص.

Tool 3 · Student engagement snapshot · لقطة تفاعل الطلاب

Every 5 minutes, count how many students are actively working on the task (writing, speaking, reading, listening with a purpose). Write the number and what the class was doing. It shows how engagement changes across stages of the lesson.

Tool 4 · The feedback conversation · حوار التغذية الراجعة

StepUseful phrases
1. The teacher reflects first"How do you think the lesson went in relation to the focus?" · "What are you most pleased with?"
2. Share evidence"At minute 12, I noticed that…" · "Eight students answered the first question; after the pair discussion, fifteen did."
3. Ask, don't tell"What was your thinking behind…?" · "What might happen if…?"
4. Agree one next step"What is one thing you will try next week?" · "How will you know it worked?"
