Module 4: Task 2 Topic Sentences & Development

Build paragraphs with a clear controlling idea, explanation and relevant example.

Strategy Lesson
  • Identify the exact task before writing.
  • Choose the most relevant information or idea.
  • Make each sentence serve a clear purpose.
  • Review for accuracy after content is clear.
Worked Task

Prompt: Governments should spend more on public transport than on new roads. To what extent do you agree?

Focus: Plan one body paragraph: topic sentence → explanation → example.
