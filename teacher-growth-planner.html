Teacher Self-Reflection & Annual Growth Planner

التأمل الذاتي وخطة النمو المهني السنوية للمعلم

Rate yourself honestly on 20 statements across five areas of practice. The planner draws your profile, points to your priority area, and helps you set up to three goals for the year with an evidence log.

قيّم نفسك بصدق في 20 عبارة موزعة على خمسة مجالات. سترى ملفك المهني ومجال الأولوية، ثم تضع حتى ثلاثة أهداف للعام مع سجل للأدلة.

🔒 Your answers stay in this browser only. Nothing is sent or saved online. · إجاباتك تبقى في متصفحك فقط.

Scale: 1 = not yet · 2 = sometimes · 3 = usually · 4 = consistently, and I could show a colleague how.

Write each goal so it is specific, measurable and realistic. Example: "By March, I will use exit tickets in at least two lessons a week with Grade 10 and adjust the next lesson using the results."
