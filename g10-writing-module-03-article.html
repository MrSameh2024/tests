Module 3 · Article Writing

Describe a Place — A Special Day at Souq Waqif

كتابة مقال وصفي عن مكان — يوم مميز في سوق واقف

Course Goal

Write a vivid, well-organised article with a catchy title and four paragraphs: introduction, features, personal impression/highlight, and recommendation. Use descriptive adjectives, one question and one exclamation.

اكتب مقالاً وصفياً منظمًا بعنوان جذاب وأربع فقرات: مقدمة، ملامح المكان، الانطباع الشخصي وأبرز تجربة، ثم التوصية، مع استخدام صفات وصفية وسؤال وتعجب.

Progress: 0 / 10 stations

Stations 1–5 are automatically marked. Stations 6–10 save your writing locally and require teacher review. Minimum progression score: 10/20.

Station 1 · Words & Meaning

Build the vocabulary

Match each word to the best meaning.

1. showing the customs of a place

2. full of bright colours

3. full of many people

4. easy to remember because it was special

Station 2 · Main Verbs

Choose precise verbs

1. Last Friday, I ___ Souq Waqif with my family.

2. We ___ the narrow streets and small shops.

3. I really ___ the traditional food.

4. I would ___ this place to visitors.

Station 3 · Linking Words

Connect ideas smoothly

1. ___, we walked through the main market.

2. ___, we stopped for a traditional meal.

3. I liked the atmosphere ___ it felt lively and authentic.

4. ___, I bought a small souvenir before leaving.

Station 4 · Reference Words

Avoid unnecessary repetition

1. Souq Waqif is in Doha. ___ is famous for its traditional atmosphere.

2. The shops sell spices and souvenirs. ___ are popular with visitors.

3. I watched a street performance. ___ was the highlight of my visit.

4. We spent two hours at the market and ate dinner ___.

Station 5 · Sentence Order

Build the article structure

Choose the best paragraph order.

Which title is most suitable?

Which sentence works best as a recommendation?

Which line adds an exclamation naturally?

Station 6 · Write the Opening

Introduction paragraph

Write 45–70 words. Introduce Souq Waqif, when you visited, who you went with and why the day was special.

Station 7 · Write the Body

Features paragraph

Write 60–90 words describing the place. Include at least three descriptive adjectives and one linking word.

Station 8 · Personal Impression

Your highlight

Write 50–80 words about the most memorable part of your visit. Include one question or one exclamation.

Station 9 · Recommendation

Write the ending

Write 35–60 words. Give a clear recommendation and explain why someone should visit.

Station 10 · Independent Writing

Write the full article

Write your complete article with a catchy title and four paragraphs. Aim for 180–230 words.
