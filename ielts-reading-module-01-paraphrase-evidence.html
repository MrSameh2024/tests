Module 1: Paraphrase Recognition & Evidence Location

Find the idea, not just the same words.

Strategy Lesson

1. What is paraphrase recognition?

IELTS Reading often expresses the same idea using different vocabulary or grammar. Strong readers learn to match meaning, not identical words.

Question: The project became more expensive than expected.
Passage: The final cost exceeded the original estimate.

Match: became more expensive → exceeded the estimate

2. Evidence Location Strategy

  1. Underline the key idea in the question.
  2. Predict possible synonyms or paraphrases.
  3. Scan for the concept, not the exact wording.
  4. Read one sentence before and after the likely evidence.
  5. Confirm that the evidence answers the exact question.
Common trap: finding the same topic but not the exact answer.
Worked Example

3. Worked Example

Passage: Many employees initially resisted remote working because they feared communication would become less effective. However, after several months, most reported that team communication had improved.

Question: Workers first believed remote work might damage communication.

Evidence: “initially resisted” + “feared communication would become less effective”

Answer: True
