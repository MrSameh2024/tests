Module 5 · Report Writing

Report with Confidence · Foreign Language Learning

كتابة تقرير منظّم

Course Goal

Write a structured report with a title and clear headings. Explain the purpose, describe languages and resources, identify learners and reasons, and recommend improvements. All model observations are fictional practice data.

اكتب تقريرًا منظمًا بعنوان وعناوين فرعية، موضحًا الهدف واللغات والموارد والمتعلمين والأسباب، ثم قدّم توصيات للتحسين. جميع بيانات التدريب افتراضية.

Progress: 0 / 10 stations

Stations 1–5 are automatically marked. Stations 6–10 save locally and require teacher review. Minimum progression score: 10/20.

Station 4 · Accuracy

Use formal grammar

1. Students ___ access to online resources.

2. The school ___ several language clubs.

3. More resources should ___ provided.

4. The report ___ based on fictional practice data.

Station 6 · Purpose

Write the purpose

Write 35–55 words explaining why the report was prepared.

Station 7 · Findings 1

Languages & resources

Write 50–80 words describing languages offered and available resources.

Station 8 · Findings 2

Learners & reasons

Write 50–80 words explaining who learns languages and why.

Station 9 · Recommendations

Suggest improvements

Write 40–70 words with two or three practical recommendations.

Station 10 · Independent Report

Write the full report

Write a complete report with a title and headings. Aim for 180–230 words.
