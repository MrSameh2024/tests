Module 3: Part 2 Cue Card Structure

Use one-minute planning to sustain a clear 1–2 minute response.

Strategy Lesson

  • Add one clear reason or explanation.
  • Support with a natural example or detail.
  • Keep speaking rather than searching for perfect wording.
