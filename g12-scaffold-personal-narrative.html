Source-based model; editable placeholders are shown in square brackets. · نموذج مستند إلى الملف مع فراغات للتخصيص

Last year, while I was sitting in my room, my friend Hamad called me and invited me to travel with volunteers to Gaza to help homeless people there. I told my father, and he let me go. When we arrived, we saw destroyed houses and the difficult lives people were living. We felt very sad and tried to help. We gave them tents, food, drinks, blankets, medicine and heaters to protect them from the cold. My experience in Gaza made me realise that we all have an important role in helping others. I felt happy to take part.
