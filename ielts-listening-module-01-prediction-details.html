Module 1: Prediction, Numbers, Names & Spelling

Predict before you listen. Catch details accurately.

توقّع قبل أن تستمع، والتقط التفاصيل بدقة.

Strategy Lesson

1. Predict the Answer Type

Before the recording starts, ask: What kind of answer fits here? A name? A number? A time? A place? A noun?

Example: “The appointment is at ______.” → predict a time.
Why it helps: prediction reduces the amount of information your brain has to process while listening.

2. High-Risk Detail Areas

  • Numbers: 15 vs 50, 13 vs 30
  • Names: listen for spelling corrections
  • Dates: distinguish day / month / year
  • Prices: catch corrected amounts
  • Spelling: one wrong letter can lose the answer
