Module 3: Matching Headings

Identify the main purpose of a paragraph rather than choosing a heading from one repeated word.

Strategy Lesson

What to do

  1. Read the paragraph for its central purpose.
  2. Ignore examples until you understand the main point.
  3. Compare headings by meaning, not vocabulary overlap.
  4. Eliminate headings that are too narrow.
Practice Passage

Urban gardens can improve neighbourhoods in several ways. They provide fresh produce, create social spaces and can even reduce summer temperatures locally. Their value therefore extends beyond food production.
