Module 4: Matching Information & Features

Track where specific people, details and characteristics appear.

Strategy

  1. Underline the unique detail in the question.
  2. Scan for names, dates or distinctive concepts.
  3. Expect paraphrase rather than exact repetition.
  4. Confirm the full sentence before matching.

Practice Passage

Dr Patel studied coastal erosion. Professor Lin focused on water quality, while Dr Evans investigated changes in fish populations.

Guided Practice

1. Who researched water quality?

2. Who studied fish populations?

Mastery Check

1. Who researched coastal erosion?

2. Who investigated water quality?

3. Who studied fish population change?
